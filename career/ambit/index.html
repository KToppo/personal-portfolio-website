Building Digital Systems at Ambit Collective

My professional journey began in 2026 when I joined Ambit Collective in the Risk Advisory Department, working as an AppSheet and Data Visualization Developer.

From the beginning, my role focused on something slightly different from traditional audit environments. Instead of operating within manual, paperwork-heavy workflows, my responsibility was to help the firm move toward structured digital systems.

Much of the operational work inside audit teams historically relies on fragmented documents, spreadsheets, and manual tracking. My goal has been to redesign these processes into integrated digital workflows that allow both internal teams and clients to access structured information in real time.

What I Work On

My work sits at the intersection of business operations and technology implementation. Rather than focusing purely on software development, the emphasis is on designing systems that make day-to-day work simpler, more reliable, and easier to manage.

Some of my primary responsibilities include:

  • Designing and deploying audit workflow applications using AppSheet
  • Converting manual documentation processes into structured digital workflows
  • Managing application users, permissions, and lifecycle maintenance
  • Building interactive analytics dashboards in Microsoft Power BI
  • Developing reporting systems that improve operational transparency and decision-making

Early Professional Experience

Within the first few months of working in the firm, I gained hands-on experience that rarely appears in classroom environments.

I worked directly with real operational processes, where the objective was not simply to write code, but to design systems that people could actually rely on.

During this early period, I developed practical experience in:

  • Real-world business application deployment
  • Data governance and user management
  • Process automation inside audit environments
  • Managing the lifecycle of internal digital tools

Lessons from the Experience

Working inside a growing firm exposed me very early to the practical realities of digital transformation.

Technology in a business environment must solve operational problems, not exist as an isolated technical exercise. Every system needs to integrate with existing workflows, support the people who use it, and improve the clarity of information rather than complicate it.

This experience has shaped how I approach technology today. Whether working with data systems, automation tools, or analytics dashboards, I focus on building solutions that are practical, maintainable, and aligned with real business needs.
